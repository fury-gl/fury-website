Let's create sphere actor to add three spheres to display.

    sphere_actor = sphere(
        np.asarray([(15, 0, 0), (0, 15, 0), (0, 0, 15)]).reshape((3, 3)),
        radii=15,
        colors=np.asarray([(1, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0), (0, 0, 1)]).reshape((3, 3)),
        phi=48,
        theta=48,
